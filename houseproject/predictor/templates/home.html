How it works

From raw data to a live prediction

1
Clean the data

Handled missing values, converted sq ft ranges, grouped rare locations and removed price outliers per location.

2
Train and tune models

Compared Ridge, Random Forest and two Gradient Boosting models using GridSearchCV and picked the best one.

3
Predict in the browser

A Django app serves the saved model with user accounts, saved history, charts and a dark mode.
